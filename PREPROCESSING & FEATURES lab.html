02 / Quick reference

The six moves.

Run the cards in order. Step 1 creates the data, and every next card continues from the previous step.

01

Cleaning

Remove HTML, URLs, emails, emojis, and unwanted symbols from customer feedback before analysis.

re.sub(pattern, '', text)
02

Normalization

Use Step 1's cleaned customer text. Lowercase it and normalize abbreviations like gr8 to great.

cleaned_text = cleaned_text.lower()
03

Tokenization

Split a customer complaint into tokens so the model can analyze each word and phrase.

tokens = nltk.word_tokenize(text)
04

Stop words

Remove common words such as the, and, and was to keep only high-value terms.

filtered_tokens = [...]
05

N-grams & Phrase detection

Multi-word phrases carry more meaning than isolated words in support text: 'late delivery', 'customer support', 'refund request'.

bigrams = list(ngrams(tokens, 2))
06

Bag of Words

Count frequency of words in customer complaints to build a text feature vector.

Counter(tokens)
07

TF-IDF weight

Highlight distinctive words in customer review text, such as refund, delay, and billing, instead of common words like the.

TfidfVectorizer()
08

Keyword extraction

Pull key phrases that summarize the real issue from customer support tickets.

keywords = ['late delivery', 'refund request']

Why might removing every stop word be a bad idea?

Some tasks need function words. In sentiment, "not good" is very different from "good". In question answering, small words can carry structure.
